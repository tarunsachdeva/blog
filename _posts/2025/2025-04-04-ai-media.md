Every major technology change has given creators new superpowers. Computers gave us new way to make, the Internet was a new way to share. What new verb will AI give?

When computers became popular, it became a new way to make media. You could make media before, but doing it with the help of a computer was 100x faster. There was immediate pushback - many creators did not recognize digital media as ‘real’. Those who embraced that media was made to be digitized (and created platforms to make that easier) pioneered new genres and business models.  From those formulas of success, many winners emerged. 

When the Internet became popular, it became a new way to share media. You could share media before, but doing it with the Internet was 100x faster. There was immediate pushback - many creators were very protective over their work being given away for free. Those who embraced that media was made to be shared (and those who created platforms to make that easier), pioneered new genres and business models. From those new formulas of success, many winners emerged.

What about AI? What is the verb that will bring new superpowers to creators? My guess  for that verb is “change”.

AI is a new way to change media. There are hundreds of ways to remix something, but doing it with a model is 100x faster. There is pushback - creators are be rightfully concerned that their work is being edited & derivatives are being generated. Those who embrace that their creations were made to be changed (and those who created platforms to maker that easier) will invent new genres and business models. From those formulas of success, many winners will emerge.

This "new superpower" is different than others. The ability to change work instantly might mean everything we interact with (in the realm of software) can become hyper personalized on our prompts and behaviours. So the ability to change is exponentially more powerful than other waves. This is new territory, and changes the relationship between people and their work, more so than ever before.
